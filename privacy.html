Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026. This covers Agent Mission Control (the free plugin and the paid kit) and this website.

The free plugin

  • Runs on your machine only. It makes no network connections and has no analytics or telemetry.
  • Keeps one file per project, .mission/mission.json, with your goal, controls, the agent's status, questions, your notes and approvals, and a log. It writes a .gitignore next to it so the file stays out of your commits.
  • Adds the mission brief and your notes and decisions to Claude's context, only in projects where you've set a mission, plus the result of each /mc command you type.
  • We never see any of it. Delete a project's .mission/ folder to remove its data.

The kit

  • Everything above, plus a local console that listens on 127.0.0.1 only and needs a private link to open.
  • Desktop notifications are shown by your operating system and don't leave your machine.
  • Phone alerts are off until you set an ntfy topic. Then each alert sends a short text (the command waiting for approval, the agent's question, or a summary) to ntfy.sh, or to the ntfy server you choose. ntfy topics are readable by anyone who knows the name, so pick one that can't be guessed. ntfy's privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Buying the kit

Polar is the merchant of record and handles payment, tax and receipts. Polar shares your order details with us (such as your name, email address and country) so we can deliver the kit and support your purchase. We keep them as long as tax and accounting rules require, and delete what we can on request. We don't sell or share them, and we don't send marketing email. Polar's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Umami Cloud for analytics. Umami uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. For each page view it records the page and its title, the site you came from, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and approximate location, plus clicks on a few of our buttons and links, such as "Get the kit" and the Copy buttons. It uses your IP address briefly to work out the location and doesn't store it. It doesn't record your name, email or anything you type. If your browser sends Do Not Track, your browser still loads the script, but the script sends nothing. On our plan, Umami keeps this data for six months, and its privacy policy covers what it receives.

GitHub may also log visits, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and requests

For questions about the plugin or the kit, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so don't include personal details there. For a request about your purchase or your personal data, open an issue titled "Privacy request" with no details in it, and we'll reply there with a private address to write to.
